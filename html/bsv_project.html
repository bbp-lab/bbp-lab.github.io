Body Image Disturbance

Our research aims to better understand how we can objectively measure and quantify body image disturbances using a digital avatar tool called Somatomap 3D.

Somatomap Body Perception Task for Measurement of Body Image Disturbance

bdd.research@camh.ca

We are looking for participants ages 18 to 40 who do not have a neurological disorder. The study includes one single visit.

Participation in the study involves:

  • Completing surverys and questionaires
  • 1 in-person visit at 250 College St

    • You will be compensated for your time should you wish to participate and complete all study visits.

    If you are interested in participating in this study, or if you would like to get more information, please contact the research team at bdd.research@camh.ca
